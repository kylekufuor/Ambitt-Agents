01 · WHAT WE'D BUILD

The Prospect Hunter

Kwame is a daily outbound agent. His one job: find small businesses with Google reviews but no website, locate a contact email, and send them a personalised cold email on Ambitt Media's behalf, complete with a sample one-pager showing what their site could look like.

He runs every morning. By the time you start your day, 10 qualified prospects have already heard from you.

02 · HOW IT WORKS

The daily flow

  1. 1

    Hunt

    Kwame searches Google Maps for businesses in your target categories (restaurants, salons, contractors, shops) that have reviews but no linked website. He pulls 10 per day.

  2. 2

    Research

    For each business, he finds a contact email from Google, their social profiles, or their listing. He notes their category, location, review count, and rating so the email feels specific.

  3. 3

    Draft

    He writes a short, personalised cold email in Ambitt Media's voice, mentioning the business by name, referencing their reviews, and attaching a sample one-pager of what their site could look like. No price discussions, ever.

  4. 4

    Your approval

    Because you've chosen supervised mode, Kwame sends you the 10 drafted emails each morning before anything goes out. You approve the batch, skip any you don't like, and he sends the rest via Gmail.

  5. 5

    Log & track

    Every prospect, email sent, and reply gets logged in Notion automatically. You always know what's in the pipeline, what's been sent, and who's responded.
