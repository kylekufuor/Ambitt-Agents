What changes, in words

The subject line carries the news. "⚠️ Ambitt alert — Fleet Health" tells you a category; "Atlas has not run in 6 days" tells you the thing. You can triage from the notification without opening it.

Severity is structural, not decorative. A 4px rail and an eyebrow word, in four fixed levels. Colour never carries the meaning alone, so it survives a colourblind reader and a dark-mode client.

Numbers are converted. 147h becomes 6 days. 2/5 becomes "2 of 5 running". Nobody should be doing arithmetic in an alert.

One action, or none. If there is something to do there is one button; if there is nothing to do it says so, which is what stops you opening it to find out.
